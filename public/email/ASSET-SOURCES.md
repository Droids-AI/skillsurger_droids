# Email icon sources

- `whatsapp-white.png`: unmodified Digital Glyph White RGB PNG from Meta's
  official WhatsApp Brand Resource Center logo pack, downloaded 25 October 2026.
  Source: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/
  Original path: `01_Glyph/01_Digital RGB/02_PNG/Digital_Glyph_White_RGB_2026.png`.
  Displayed at 24 × 24 pixels, preserving its proportions and original colour.
- `calendar-blue.png`: PNG rendering of the existing `CalendarDays` icon from
  the project's `lucide-react` dependency (ISC licence), in Skillsurger blue.
  Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/calendar-days

Email markup uses raster icons because inline SVG is not consistently displayed
by email clients. Text labels and normal links remain usable with images blocked.
